Good To Know

Before you go

Groups & capacity

Ezemvelo requires groups of at least 3 people, and our whole group is capped at 30 hikers. Stay on the marked trail at all times.

Weather

The trail stays in the "Little Berg" below 2,100m, but Drakensberg weather turns quickly — pack warm and waterproof layers even in summer.

Water

Huts have running water, and it's generally safe to drink from streams higher up the mountain — treat it if you prefer to be cautious.

Not circular

This is a point-to-point trail. We'll organise transport pickup at Winterhoek Hut to bring everyone back to the start.

4
Preparation

What to bring

Huts give you a bed (except Mzimkhulwana) and a roof — everything else comes with you.

Sleep & carry

  • Sleeping bag (warm — high altitude nights)
  • Sleeping mat (essential for Mzimkhulwana Hut)
  • Backpack (40–60L)
  • Headlamp + spare batteries

Food & water

  • Trail meals for each night
  • Lightweight cooking gear
  • Water bottle / bladder (2L+)
  • Snacks & energy food

On the trail

  • Hiking boots, broken in
  • Warm layers + waterproof jacket
  • Sunscreen, hat & sunglasses
  • Basic first aid kit
